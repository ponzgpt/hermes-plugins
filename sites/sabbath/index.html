🕯 Sabbath · a Hermes Agent plugin

Your agent never rests. Now it rests with you.

Once a week, Hermes keeps a day of rest with you.

Goodnight
At the hour you choose, Hermes writes to your phone: the week's work is done.
Its jobs sleep
Hermes pauses its own scheduled jobs, keeps you company in anything but work, and sets aside what can wait.
One gentle digest
When the rest ends, you get what waited, in one message. Something truly urgent? /sabbath ox lifts the rest for an hour.
hermes plugins install ponzgpt/sabbath --enable
