☤ Psychopomp · a Hermes Agent plugin

Every week, your Hermes helps you write a page of your life.

If you ever go silent for good, it carries your words to the people you love.

Your Book
You live and talk to Hermes; once a week it drafts a page in your voice. Nothing is kept until you approve it.
Sealed letters
For the people you love. Once a letter is sealed, not even Hermes can read it again.
The watch
Silence, check-ins, then a code from your witness. A timer alone never sends anything. Rehearse the whole thing in 20 seconds.
